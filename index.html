What Will the Proposed ₹4,000 Crore Corridor Mean for the Noida - Greater Noida Expressway?

The proposed ₹4,000 crore elevated corridor could significantly improve connectivity across Noida and Greater Noida. The project aims to provide an alternative commuting route, reduce pressure on existing roads and improve access towards the upcoming Noida International Airport.

For buyers exploring the Noida - Greater Noida Expressway, this development could strengthen the appeal of strategically located residential areas. Sector 150, in particular, is attracting attention for its spacious homes, planned surroundings and growing interest from both end-users and property investors.

Understanding the Proposed ₹4,000 Crore Corridor

The proposed eight-lane elevated corridor will cover approximately 50 kilometres, connecting Sector 94 in Noida to Sector Chi-V in Greater Noida. The estimated project cost is ₹4,000 crore, with NHAI preparing the Detailed Project Report (DPR).

The proposed route will run alongside Yamuna Pushta Road, cross the Hindon River and connect towards the Yamuna Expressway and Faridabad-Jewar Greenfield Expressway. Approximately 27 kilometres, from Sector 94 to Sector 150, will fall under the Noida Authority's jurisdiction.

The project remains at the planning stage, and its construction timeline will depend on approvals and implementation.

How Better Connectivity Could Influence Residential Demand in Noida - Greater Noida Expressway

Improved infrastructure can make residential locations more accessible and attractive to homebuyers. The proposed corridor could offer several advantages:

  • Alternative commuting routes: Additional connectivity could reduce dependence on existing roads.
  • Airport accessibility: Better links towards Noida International Airport could benefit professionals and frequent travellers.
  • Regional connectivity: Connections towards Greater Noida and Faridabad could improve access to commercial destinations.
  • Residential demand: Improved accessibility could increase interest in emerging residential neighbourhoods.

However, proposed infrastructure should not be treated as a guarantee of property appreciation.

Why Noida Expressway Property is Attracting Homebuyers and Investors

Sector 150 is gaining attention for its combination of connectivity, open spaces and premium residential developments. Its location and proximity to major road networks make it relevant for families seeking spacious homes and investors evaluating long-term opportunities.

According to Magicbricks’ Q3 2026 property rate data, the average asking price of multi-storey apartments in Sector 150, Noida, reached approximately ₹13,254 per sq. ft., recording a 2% quarter-on-quarter increase.

For end-users, spacious configurations and recreational facilities are important considerations. Investors, meanwhile, may find the combination of existing connectivity and proposed infrastructure worth evaluating, although future returns remain uncertain.
